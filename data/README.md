# Experiment data

- `generated_candidates.json` contains the eight prompts, three generated candidates per prompt, and Python-computed structural signals saved from the experiment.
- `evaluation_scores.csv` contains the per-prompt final scores and structural signals for all 24 candidates. The final-score values are the previously collected LLM-as-a-Judge results.

The dataset is small and exploratory. Do not interpret LLM-judge scores as human ground truth.
